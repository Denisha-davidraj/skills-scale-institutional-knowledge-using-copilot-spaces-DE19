# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This README is the entry point for OctoAcme's project management process documentation. Our delivery model follows a full lifecycle: **initiation** to define the problem, goals, stakeholders, and go/no-go criteria; **planning** to turn approved work into prioritized backlog items, milestones, and risk-aware delivery plans; **execution** to deliver in small increments with clear tracking; **release** to deploy safely with verification and communication; and **retrospective** to capture lessons learned and improve future cycles.

Project execution is shared across clearly defined roles. The **Project Manager** coordinates schedules, risks, dependencies, and status communications. The **Product Manager** defines outcomes, prioritizes the roadmap and backlog, and validates business impact. **Developers** design and implement changes, maintain tests, and contribute technical risk assessments. **QA** ensures acceptance criteria are met through test strategy, validation, and release-readiness checks.

Communication is intentional and recurring to keep delivery predictable. Teams use **daily standups** to surface progress and blockers, **weekly syncs** to review risks, dependencies, and milestone health, and regular **stakeholder updates** to align on progress, decisions, and asks. Escalation paths are documented so delivery and incident communication can move quickly when needed.

Quality is built into each phase instead of deferred to the end. OctoAcme expects clear acceptance criteria, passing CI checks, and appropriate test coverage for the change type, including unit, integration, and critical-flow smoke tests before release. Security scanning and post-deployment verification are part of release readiness, and retrospectives convert outcomes into measurable improvement actions.
